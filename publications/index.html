  1. JMST
    Optical characterization of pyrromethene dyes as seeded tracer for fuel PLIF in high temperature
    Hyunchang Lee
    Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology, 2026
    Abs DOI Bib

    Accurate measurement of liquid fuel distribution is essential for optimizing injector design in liquid-fueled engines. This study investigated a pyrromethene-based fluorescent dye dissolved in exoTHDCPD as a candidate for use as a seeded tracer for PLIF diagnostics. The dyes were optically characterized using a 532 nm CW laser. Fluorescence spectra and intensities were measured in a heated cuvette setup using a spectrometer and sCMOS camera. Among the tested dyes, pyrromethene 567 exhibited nearly five times the signal intensity of the reference up to 160 degC. Using pyrromethene 567 with a 2.15 W CW laser and a 0.2 ms exposure time, the model estimates a measurement precision better than 1 percent under a beam height of 9.8 mm and thickness of 0.25 mm.

  1. IJHMT
    Uncertainty measurement of two color two dye laser-induced fluorescence thermometry and application thereof to superheated liquid jet
    Hyunchang Lee
    International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, 2025
    Abs DOI Bib

    Flash boiling spray is highly interesting in various industries, and bubble generation and growth by phase change and heat transfer in a metastable state governs the development of the spray. In this experimental study, the performance of thermometry using fluorescein disodium and sulforhodamine was evaluated using a jet of water at various injection temperatures from 25 to 84.5 degC. The measured precision was around 2 percent over 60 degC. The temperature fields of superheated liquid jet disintegration regimes were measured and their physical plausibility discussed.
